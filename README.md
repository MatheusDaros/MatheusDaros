## 🎓 Teaching AI and Web3 Bootcamps since 2020

I teach students to build Decentralized AI applications through Bootcamps, Workshops and Hands-On challenges like Hackathons and Demo Days.

The focus is practical: understand the tools, write the code, experiment, and build a working project—not just watch someone else do it.

### Inside our programming

- LLM fundamentals, transformers, GPTs, and OpenAI APIs.
- Prompt engineering and model configuration.
- AI chat applications with React and Vercel.
- Running language models locally.
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), assistants, and fine-tuning.
- Image generation, Stable Diffusion, and computer vision.
- Guest workshops and a final week dedicated to student projects.

Students follow hands-on lessons, tackle weekend projects, work with peers, and build a final project to complete the program.

## 🌎 Bringing builders together at Vibe Space

I run Vibe Space: a free, always-open AI accelerator and community for builders, generalists, and founders.

We bring people together through workshops, hackathons, meetups, and project showcases—with a focus on learning by building.

Vibe Space connects 200,000+ builders worldwide. No application to join the accelerator, no fees, and no equity required.

### Events across the AI ecosystem

Our programming includes:

- Cognition / Devin — developer meetups and virtual workshops on AI-assisted software development.
- Lovable + Oracle — meetups bringing AI-powered MVP building and cloud infrastructure into the same room.
- Clerk + Cognition — hands-on sessions such as “From Prompt to Login,” focused on building authentication.
- Community hackathons — including DevDay Exchange Community Hackathon: São Paulo.

I bring developers, founders, speakers, and technology teams together to share what they're building and help more people get started.
